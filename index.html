Project \

Yasha & Yuna

Year \ 2026
Role \ Lead Animator, Character Modeler, Rigging Artist
Software \ Autodesk Maya, Substance Painter, After Effects, Procreate

Working alongside my amazing 4-member team at KAVA Studio, Yasha & Yuna was our final year capstone project celebrating the warm nostalgia of 2010s childhoods and traditional games like batu seremban.

To reflect true authentic cultural diversity, we paired Yasha (Indian) and Yuna (Malay) as competitive best friends navigating their vibrant school days together. As the lead animator, rigging artist, and 3D character modeler, I utilized Autodesk Maya, Substance Painter, After Effects, and Procreate to bring their world, comedic facial expressions, and dynamic story to life.

Cyber-Kitsune: Dual-Form Rig

ARCHIVE DOSSIER: YASHA & YUNA

Complete 48-Panel Storyboard & Capstone Reel

🎬 Film Synopsis

Set in a 2010s Malaysian classroom, Yuna and Yasha turn a friendly recess game of batu seremban into an epic, cinematic anime-style rivalry filled with slow-motion tosses, comedic expressions, and touching moments of camaraderie.

🛠️ Technical Execution

Modeled in Autodesk Maya with clean edge loops for facial deformation. Texturing accomplished in Substance 3D Painter using stylized hand-painted maps. Final composition rendered in Arnold and composited in After Effects.
